


Input: [1, 2, 3, 4]

## Prefix

Prefix means multiplying all numbers before the current index.

| Index | Calculation | Prefix |

| 0 | 1 | 1 |
| 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 1 * 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 1 * 2 * 3 | 6 |

Prefix array = [1, 1, 2, 6]

## Suffix

Suffix means multiplying all numbers after the current index.

| Index | Calculation | Suffix |

| 0 | 2 * 3 * 4 | 24 |
| 1 | 3 * 4 | 12 |
| 2 | 4 | 4 |
| 3 | Nothing after it, so 1 | 1 |

Suffix array = [24, 12, 4, 1]

## Final Result

For each index, I multiply its prefix by its suffix.

| Index | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 * 24 | 24 |
| 1 | 1 * 12 | 12 |
| 2 | 2 * 4 | 8 |
| 3 | 6 * 1 | 6 |

Output = [24, 12, 8, 6]

Time Complexity: O(n)
Space Complexity: O(n)
